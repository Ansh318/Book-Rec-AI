# Defining the Scope of Librarian 

    1. Answer queries about books, articles, or resources 
    2. Provide recommendations based on user preferences and historical database
    3. Information retrieval from a library of documents 
    4. Assist with tasks such as categorizing books, answer FAQs, and guiding the user on how to find content 
    5. Monitor Payment History of user, active balances
    6. Offer deals/rewards on books
